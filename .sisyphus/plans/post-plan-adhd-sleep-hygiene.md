### Outline

**I. Introduction (150 words)**
- Hook: The vicious cycle of ADHD and poor sleep
- Statistic: 50-75% of adults with ADHD have sleep issues
- Promise: Science-backed strategies that actually work

**II. Why ADHD Brains Struggle with Sleep (300 words)**
- The delayed sleep phase (circadian rhythm differences)
- Racing thoughts and mental hyperactivity
- Dopamine dysregulation at night
- The "revenge bedtime procrastination" phenomenon
- Link to existing post: Time Blindness ("not now" vs "now" applies to sleep too)

**III. The Cost of Poor Sleep (200 words)**
- Exacerbated ADHD symptoms
- Executive function decline
- Emotional dysregulation
- Reference existing: Life Expectancy post (sleep as first-order factor)

**IV. Sleep Hygiene Strategies That Work (400 words)**
- **Environment**: Temperature, darkness, noise (concrete specs)
- **Routine**: Same wake time, wind-down ritual (30-60 min)
- **Stimulant timing**: When to stop caffeine
- **Screen management**: Blue light + dopamine triggers
- **Mindfulness tools**: Body scan, 4-7-8 breathing
- **Melatonin**: Evidence-based usage (0.5-3mg, timing)
- **Exercise timing**: Morning vs evening

**V. When to Seek Professional Help (150 words)**
- Sleep apnea screening
- Restless leg syndrome
- CBT-I (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia)

**VI. Conclusion (100 words)**
- 3 key takeaways
- Actionable first step
- Engagement question
