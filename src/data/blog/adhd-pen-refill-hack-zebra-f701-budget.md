### Why Budget Pen Refills for the ADHD Brain Actually Matter

Budget pen refills for the ADHD brain are not a "nice to have" — they are damage control for a very specific ADHD pattern: buying the same cheap consumable over and over because the last one got lost, binned with a dead pen, or left in a jacket that went to the wash.

### The ADHD Pen Refill Hack, Step by Step

The ADHD pen refill hack works by borrowing the ink cartridge from a cheap generic pen instead of buying the official Zebra F-Refill. I use the **ZYM Free Horse HO-309**, a 0.7mm retractable ballpoint typically sold in a ten-pack for well under $10 — meaning each pen, refill included, costs a fraction of a single official Zebra refill.

#### What You Need

- One [ZYM Free Horse HO-309](https://www.amazon.com/ZYM-Free-Horse-Retractable-Ball-Point/dp/B012OA8JYM) (or any similar cheap 0.7mm click pen — the pack of ten means you have nine spares before you need to think about this again)
- Your empty [Zebra F-701](/posts/zebra-f701-the-perfect-pen-for-adhd-brain-dump-journaling/)
- Small scissors or a hobby knife
- A genuine Zebra refill (empty is fine) to measure against, if you have one lying around

#### The Steps

1. **Disassemble the ZYM pen** and pull out its ink cartridge — the full tube, tip included.
2. **Lay it next to a genuine Zebra F-Refill** (or the F-701's original spent cartridge) and mark where the ZYM tube needs to end to match the length.
3. **Trim the tube** at the back end only — the empty plastic shaft, never anywhere near the ink chamber or the tip. Cut conservatively; you can always take off more, but you cannot put the tube back once it is gone.
4. **Test-fit it** in the F-701 barrel before you commit. If the tip doesn't sit flush against the front of the pen when the mechanism is clicked out, trim a sliver more and test again.
5. **Reassemble the F-701** and click it a few times to seat the refill properly.

> **ADHD m8 pro-tip**: Do this with the TV on or a podcast running. It's a five-minute fiddly task, which is exactly the kind of task ADHD brains stall out on if it's the only thing happening. Pair it with something else and it stops feeling like a chore.

### The Honest Trade-offs

This is a budget pen refill hack for ADHD brains who want cheap over polished, so it comes with real trade-offs — no pretending otherwise:

- **Writing quality is a lottery.** Generic cartridges vary between pens and even between individual units in the same pack. Some write as smoothly as the stock Zebra refill; some are scratchier.
- **No archival or waterproof claims.** Unlike the [Zebra JK gel refill](/posts/zebra-f701-refill-guide-upgrade-your-writing-experience/), you have no guarantee about ink permanence.
- **It's a DIY mod, not a supported product.** If you cut through the ink chamber, that cartridge is done — and possibly your desk, if it leaks.
- **You're trading consistency for cost.** If your F-701 is a daily-carry tool you rely on for archive-quality journaling, this isn't the refill for that job — see the [refill guide](/posts/zebra-f701-refill-guide-upgrade-your-writing-experience/) for that use case instead.

A cheap alternative to the Zebra F-701 refill is worth it specifically when the cost or hassle of restocking official refills is the thing stopping you from using the pen at all.

**This hack is for you if:**

- You lose or misplace pens often enough that per-refill cost actually matters
