### Why Refill Choice Matters for ADHD

The ADHD brain notices friction. Tiny irritations that neurotypical writers ignore become full stop-blocks for us.

**Stock ballpoint ink can be:**
- **Scratchy** → requires more pressure → hand fatigue → shorter writing sessions
- **Inconsistent** → skipping forces re-tracing → breaks flow → thoughts evaporate
- **Slow-drying** → smudges when you flip pages → frustration → quit journaling

The right refill removes these barriers. It makes writing **effortless**, which means you are more likely to actually do it.

When your pen glides across paper like butter, you write longer. When ink dries instantly, you stop worrying about smudges. When lines are crisp and dark, your brain rewards you with dopamine.

**Tool optimization reduces activation energy.** Lower activation energy means higher follow-through.

### How to Swap the Refill (30 Seconds)

The F-701 makes this embarrassingly easy:

1. **Unscrew** the barrel from the grip section (twist counterclockwise)
2. **Remove** the old refill by pulling it straight out
3. **Insert** the new refill into the barrel (check orientation — the writing tip goes toward the front)
4. **Screw** the barrel back onto the grip section
5. **Click** the mechanism a few times to seat the refill

**Pro tip**: If the new refill feels loose or wobbly, wrap a small strip of tape around the cartridge barrel near the tip. This creates a friction fit that eliminates any rattling.
