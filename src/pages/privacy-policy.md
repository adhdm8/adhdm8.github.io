*Last updated: July 26, 2026*

ADHD m8 ("we," "us," or "this site") respects your privacy. This page explains what information is collected when you visit **adhdm8.com**, how it's used, and what choices you have.

### What We Collect

We do not require accounts, logins, or sign-ups to read this site, and we do not ask you to submit personal or health information. The self-screening quiz on this site is scored entirely in your browser — your answers are never sent to us or stored anywhere.

The information we do collect comes from standard website analytics:

- **Usage data**: pages visited, time on page, referring site, approximate location (derived from IP address), and device/browser type, collected via **Google Analytics (GA4)**.
- **Local storage**: your light/dark theme preference is saved in your browser's local storage, not sent to us.

### Cookies and Third-Party Services

- **Google Analytics** sets cookies to distinguish visitors and measure site usage. You can opt out using the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings.
- **Amazon Associates**: some links to books and products on this site are affiliate links. If you click one, Amazon may set its own cookies to track the referral. See our [Disclaimer](/disclaimer/) for details.
- **Instagram**: links to our Instagram profile are subject to Instagram's own privacy policy once you leave this site.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties beyond the analytics and affiliate providers named above, and we do not use your data for advertising profiles of our own.

### Children's Privacy

This site is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

### Your Choices

You can:

- Use your browser's "Do Not Track" or cookie-blocking settings.
- Opt out of Google Analytics tracking (link above).
- Contact us to ask what data, if any, is associated with you.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves — for example, if we add advertising or new third-party tools. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
